A friend asked for a box to keep her grandmother's buttons in. I had a pile of
walnut offcuts, so I made one from those.

The sides are mitred and held with a spline. The lid is the hard part: it has
to close with a quiet click, and wood moves with the seasons, so I left a gap
about as thick as a sheet of paper all the way round.

I finished it with oil, which smells wonderful and takes a weekend to cure.
