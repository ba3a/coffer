Kerfcalc answers the questions I used to scribble on offcuts: how much wood
does the blade turn into dust, how far apart do the pins go, and will the cut
list fit on the board I have.

```sh
kerfcalc waste --board 2400 --kerf 2.4 --pieces 600,450,450,300
```

I wrote it while building the [walnut keepsake box](../../journal/walnut-box/).
It shares a workshop with the game [Tenon Tumble](project:tenon-tumble).
